2024-25｜Charter Year

Starting with the founders' meeting in August 2024, the provisional club gathered time after time and was formally chartered in November. In its first year it held joint meetings, a music recital, and an annual thanksgiving dinner, and everyone went from getting to know one another to working together.

2025-26｜Deepening Year

In the second year, regular meetings became a classroom for sustainability learning: ESG, carbon inventory, the circular economy, and AI applications took turns. From the first anniversary celebration, becoming a Peacebuilder Club, and the Women's Impact Forum, to volunteering and the run at the Rotary International Convention in Taipei, the Members were there all the way.
