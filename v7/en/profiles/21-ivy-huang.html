Opening Scene｜After a Turning Point in Life

Dr. Ivy once shared in an interview that her experience after the 921 earthquake gave her a new understanding of scent and companionship. In the most chaotic days, a familiar smell may be the small bit of strength that helps a person settle down.

Since then, she has kept learning, teaching, and practicing, for twenty-eight years.

That starting point

Beyond whether it smells good, a scent is also tied to a person's experience, present feelings, and memories of life.

Professional Background｜The "Bespoke Spirit" of the West Market in Fucheng

She often mentions the century-old West Market in Fucheng, Tainan. There, a bespoke tailor does not hand the same ready-made suit to everyone; he first understands a person's figure, needs, and life, and then carefully adjusts it stitch by stitch.

She brings this spirit into the world of scent: she avoids defining a person with a fixed answer, and starts from smelling, memory, setting, and story to gradually find the scent direction that belongs to the moment. Her teaching philosophy is the same sentence: "Explain complex knowledge clearly, and keep the human warmth in learning."

Main Line of Impact｜One Herb Becomes an Industry Chain

What she has invested in most deeply in recent years is a sustainable path that starts from the land. Sifan Natural Farm links NPOs and social welfare groups across Taiwan, letting partners with different conditions take part in raising seedlings, planting, tending, and harvesting herbs. After professional extraction and analysis of the herb materials, Dr. Ivy develops the formulas and designs the products. Finally, companies join through talks, employee scent blending, purchasing, and co-creation, extending support from a one-time event into ongoing collaboration.

Her role in the industry chain

Turning the story of the raw materials into collaboration plans that companies and the general public can understand and take part in.

Another Branch｜Bringing Scent into Family, Emotional, and Life Education

Beyond the classroom and her brand, she has long worked with public agencies, schools, and professional institutions, using the five senses and aroma to accompany different people:

  • Family education: couples' talks at the Tainan City Family Education Center, parenting education and parent-child life education in Chiayi City, and talks on transnational marriage families for the National Immigration Agency, Ministry of the Interior.
  • Workplace stress relief: employee welfare talks for the Labor Affairs Bureau, Tainan City Government.
  • Universities and youth: the ALOHA Sustainable Development Winter Camp and Seed Teacher Training at National Sun Yat-sen University, and a five senses and self-awareness workshop; she mentored a student aroma startup team at National Chiayi University, which won the Ministry of Education Food Innovation Silver Award.
  • Elder care: "Intimacy in Later Life, Companionship for the Long Run" at National Chiayi University, senior health care workshops at Shu-Jen Home Economics and Commercial Vocational High School, and healthy aging aesthetics at National Tainan Junior College of Nursing.
  • Collaboration with psychology professionals: the Chiayi City Clinical Psychologists Association, Taichung Veterans General Hospital Chiayi Branch, and Hehao Psychotherapy Clinic.

In 2024, she was also recognized by Chiayi City as an outstanding individual in family education for Grandparents' Day.

International Recognition｜Taking a Seat on the World Cup Judging Panel

In 2016, she was invited to serve as a judge at the OMC Hairdressing and Beauty World Cup and was selected as Best Judge; the same year, she also won double championships at an international hairdressing and beauty competition in Korea. This international experience added a cross-border perspective to her aroma education.

In brand collaborations, she designed a one-day immersive scent-blending course for The Lalu Sun Moon Lake, and has worked with brands and venues including Ai En Si Yachts, Fu Guang Construction, Jin Feng Group, Cheng Shi Design, and Shui Wan Restaurant, turning brand stories into scent memories that can be felt.

Her Place in the Club｜Giving Vocational Service a Gentle Scent

At the 32nd online regular meeting on January 19, 2026, she shared with the Members on the topic "Women's Inner Aura: Using Essential Oil Fragrance to Build Your Confidence and Workplace Charm," bringing aroma education into the Club's regular meeting.

The Club believes in "serving society through our professions." Dr. Ivy's herb industry chain happens to connect vocational service, community service, and social welfare participation on the same line: social welfare partners gain work and a role, companies gain a concrete way to take part in sustainability, and scent becomes an entry point everyone can experience firsthand.

What she brings to the Club

Turning expertise into experiences that can be felt and joined, bringing service into everyone's life.

Closing｜Scent Is an Entry Point to Begin Feeling

For Dr. Ivy, scent does not need to give a standard answer. From a person's experience of smell to the impression a brand hopes to leave; from an introductory class to professional training that leads to independent judgment, she is still walking this path.

What she blends is scent; what she leaves behind is a warmth, well remembered, between people, and between people and the land.
